FIELD GUIDE • EMERALD FRONTIER

Adventurer's Handbook

Your mission is to protect the Emerald Keep from the army emerging through the Dark Gate. Build wisely, evolve your defenders, and survive every assault.

01

Choose Your Defenders

Select a unit card, then tap an empty tile on one of the five lanes. Each defender has a different role, cost, and attack style.

02

Manage Your Energy

Energy regenerates over time and defeating attackers rewards more. Save for stronger units, but build a balanced defense across all lanes.

03

Upgrade & Specialize

Tap Upgrade, then select a deployed defender to evolve it. Higher tiers improve power and unlock specializations. Elite defenders unlock as you progress.

04

Survive the Waves

Enemy armies grow larger every wave and appear in random lanes. Watch for fast, armored, poisonous, and heavy attackers. Stop them before they reach the Keep.

05

Defeat Bosses & Ascend

Every fifth wave is a boss encounter. After defeating it, tap one deployed defender for a free Ascension—or skip the reward.

06

Answer the Challenges

Answer knowledge questions when they appear. Boss encounters also trigger a special challenge. Read carefully and use rewards to strengthen your defense.

07

Protect Every Lane

Each lane has three lives. When attackers break through, that lane loses a life. If your defenses collapse, the Emerald Keep is in danger.

08

Battle Controls

Use Remove to clear a deployed defender (no energy refund). Use sound and music buttons to adjust audio. On phones, rotate to landscape.
